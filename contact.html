Olympic Capital

Visiting Lausanne Headquarters

The Federation's executive administrative offices are situated within the prestigious Maison du Sport International (MSI) in Lausanne, Switzerland—the globally recognized Olympic Capital housing over 30 international sports federations.

Geneva Airport (GVA) 45 mins direct rail connection to Lausanne Gare
Lausanne Central Station Metro M2 to Délices or Bus 1 / 25 to Av. de Rhodanie

Visitor Protocol: Building security access requires advance delegation registration. Please present a valid passport or government ID at the main MSI reception desk.
