Our Vision & Mandate

Governing with Integrity, Inspiring Generations

Founded in 1968, the International Sports Federation represents 142 national member associations across five continental zones. We are recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC) as the sole worldwide governing authority.

Our primary mission is to promote, organize, and develop international championships, ensure strict adherence to anti-doping codes, and empower athletes from grassroots to the podium.

Olympic Excellence

Delivering premier world championships and pathways to the Olympic Games.

Integrity & Fair Play

Zero tolerance for doping, discrimination, or manipulation of competitions.

Global Development

Investing in coaching academies, youth pathways, and emerging nations.
